Privacy

Wornways makes videos in your browser. Files you add are read on this device and saved in your browser’s activity library. You can remove individual activities or choose Delete saved activities on the Source screen.

Connecting Strava

When you authorize Wornways, its server exchanges the authorization code for a token and requests the activity IDs, names, dates, sports, distances, start and finish locations, and GPS, timing, and elevation data needed for your replay. The server can read the token from an encrypted sign-in cookie and forwards account and activity deletion notifications to the open session. Wornways caches the activity list and downloaded tracks in encrypted form in your browser for the same session as the sign-in cookie, up to two hours. On reload, Wornways checks the list against Strava and reuses tracks that still match. Ending the session clears the cache. If the browser was closed, Wornways removes expired entries the next time you open it. The file library contains activities you added as files.

To revoke access, choose Disconnect Strava on the Source screen or remove Wornways in Strava settings. Strava may collect and use information about use of its API to operate, support, and improve its services. Strava’s privacy policy describes its handling of personal data.

Connecting Garmin Connect

On a personal Wornways installation you control, a Garmin Connect Personal API key is sent through that installation to Garmin to obtain an access token. Wornways does not save the key. Activity list and file requests also pass through that installation. The browser keeps the token, activity summaries, and downloaded routes in this tab’s memory for up to two hours. Disconnecting or reloading clears the session. To revoke the key itself, delete it in Garmin Connect settings.

Maps and exports

OpenFreeMap receives requests for the map tiles needed to show the replay area. Download saves an MP4 to your device. Share hands the file to the app you choose. Wornways does not host your videos.

To contact Wornways about your data, use the project’s support page. Please keep activity files and access tokens out of public reports.
